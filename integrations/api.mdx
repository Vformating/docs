The Readyt API gives you programmatic access to everything your Readyt account tracks — brand visibility scores, AI citation data, Reddit lead opportunities, and competitor benchmarks. Use it to embed Readyt data into your internal dashboards, feed your CRM with intent-scored leads, fire Slack alerts when opportunities surface, or build multi-step automations in n8n or Zapier. If your team works outside the Readyt dashboard, the API lets you bring the data to wherever you already work.

## Authentication

Readyt uses API key authentication. Every request must include your key in the `Authorization` header as a Bearer token. Retrieve your API key from your Readyt dashboard under **Settings → API**.

```bash
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  https://api.readyt.ai/v1/brand/visibility
```

Keep your API key private — treat it like a password. If a key is compromised, rotate it immediately from the **Settings → API** page in your dashboard.

## What the API exposes

The Readyt API surfaces the same data layers you see in the dashboard, all accessible as structured JSON responses:

- **Brand visibility scores** — query your visibility rating broken down by AI model (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini), region, and time range
- **Source visibility and citation data** — see which Reddit threads and posts are generating AI citations for your brand, including citation frequency and recency
- **Reddit lead opportunities** — retrieve a filterable list of high-intent threads scored by purchase intent, so you can prioritize outreach programmatically
- **Competitor visibility comparisons** — pull side-by-side citation and visibility metrics for any competitor you track in Readyt
- **Reply and post performance metrics** — access engagement data on your Readyt-assisted replies and posts, including upvote trends and click-through rates
- **Backlink tracking data** — retrieve backlink activity associated with Reddit mentions that are influencing your AI model citations

## Rate limits

Rate limits vary depending on your Readyt plan. Check your current usage and limits in the **Settings → API** section of your dashboard, or contact [support@readyt.ai](mailto:support@readyt.ai) if you need higher throughput for a specific integration.

<Tip>
  For AI-native workflows where you want to query Readyt data in natural language, use the [MCP integration](/integrations/mcp) instead of raw API calls — it lets Claude or Cursor ask questions about your brand visibility and leads directly in chat.
</Tip>
