## Why Reddit bans accounts

Reddit's moderation systems — both automated and human — watch for a consistent set of signals that flag accounts as inauthentic. Understanding what triggers a ban is the first step to avoiding one.

- **New accounts posting immediately.** Reddit treats zero-history accounts that jump straight into posting or commenting as spam bots. Accounts need karma, age, and community participation before they can post reliably in most subreddits.
- **Unnatural activity patterns.** Posting at regular intervals, performing actions in rapid succession, or spiking activity suddenly all look machine-generated to Reddit's detection systems.
- **Promotional-sounding content.** Replies that read like advertisements — product pitches, unnatural keyword density, or obvious CTAs — get flagged by moderators and AutoModerator rules.
- **No community history.** Accounts that only ever post about one brand or topic, with no normal browsing or voting behavior, stand out as inauthentic even if individual posts look fine in isolation.

These are exactly the patterns Readyt's Anti-Ban Engine is designed to avoid at every layer of the stack.

## Best practices

The Anti-Ban Engine handles the heavy lifting automatically, but a few habits on your end will maximize account health and keep your campaigns running smoothly.

- **Start with manual approval mode** to review every reply before it posts — this lets you catch tone issues before they cause removals.
- **Let ReadytWarm run for at least 2 weeks** before enabling auto-post on a new account; rushing warmup is the most common cause of early bans.
- **Keep daily posting volume within your plan's recommended range** — the dashboard shows your safe posting ceiling per account.
- **Avoid overly promotional reply language** — Readyt drafts replies to sound like genuine community members, but review your replies to make sure they match the tone of each subreddit.
- **Monitor Account Health Scores in your dashboard weekly** so you can catch a yellowing account before it goes red.
