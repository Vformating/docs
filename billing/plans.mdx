## Choosing a plan

Picking the right plan comes down to how central Reddit is to your acquisition strategy and how much automation you need to run it efficiently.

**Starter** is the best starting point for solo founders or small teams testing Reddit as a growth channel for the first time. You get 150 AI-guided replies per month, 25 custom keywords, and weekly lead tracking refreshes — enough to establish a presence, learn which subreddits convert, and validate the channel before committing to a higher volume.

**Growth** is the right fit for growth teams that want tighter feedback loops and deeper audience insight. Daily lead and competitor tracking refreshes mean you're never working from stale data, and sentiment analysis lets you understand how your brand is landing in conversations — not just whether you're showing up.

**Pro** is built for teams running Reddit as a primary acquisition channel. With 1,000 AI-guided replies per month, 500 daily auto DMs, real-time tracking, and brand mentions monitoring, you have everything you need to treat Reddit like a full GTM motion. The included 30-minute onboarding call helps you configure the platform for maximum impact from day one.

**Agency** is designed for marketing agencies managing Reddit growth across multiple brands, or larger SaaS companies that want a fully managed, done-for-you service with guaranteed reach. Readyt's team handles everything — strategy, writing, posting, and reporting — while you focus on results.

## Yearly vs. monthly billing

Every paid plan is available on a monthly or yearly billing cycle. Choosing yearly saves you **20%** compared to the monthly rate — and locks in that price for the full 12 months, so you won't be affected by any price changes during your subscription term.

All yearly plans include the following bonuses on top of the standard plan features:

- **Reddit Mastery Course** ($297 value) — a structured course covering subreddit selection, comment strategy, and community-safe growth tactics, included with every yearly plan
- **DWY Onboarding Pack** ($1,000 value) — a done-with-you onboarding session to build out your keyword strategy, competitor tracking, and reply templates; available on yearly Pro and above
- **12-month price lock** — your rate is guaranteed for the full billing period, regardless of plan price changes
- **Priority beta access** — get early access to new Readyt features before they roll out to the general user base

## Upgrading or downgrading

You can change your plan at any time from your account billing settings. Upgrades take effect immediately — your new reply limits, seat count, and feature access are available as soon as the change is confirmed. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, so you keep your current plan's features until the period you've already paid for ends.
