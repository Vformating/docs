Rather than using the platform yourself, you hand the strategy and execution to Readyt's specialists, who build your playbook, write your replies, run your DM outreach, and deliver weekly reports showing exactly what the work is producing.

## Guaranteed reach explained

The reach guarantee is a commitment to a minimum number of total Reddit post impressions across the subreddits Readyt operates in on your behalf during your engagement period. On a 3-month contract, Readyt guarantees at least **3 million impressions**. On a 6-month contract, that figure rises to **6 million impressions**.

These impressions are generated through a combination of direct replies in high-traffic threads, original posts in relevant subreddits, and community engagement that surfaces your brand in front of readers who are already discussing problems your product solves. Readyt tracks impression counts throughout the engagement and surfaces them in your weekly reports so you always have full visibility.

If the guaranteed reach figure is not met by the end of your engagement period, contact your dedicated account manager or reach out to [support@readyt.ai](mailto:support@readyt.ai) to discuss how it will be addressed.

## Anti-Ban Premium accounts

Reddit moderation is one of the biggest risks in any Reddit marketing program. Accounts that look new, act at high velocity, or post content that triggers spam filters can be shadowbanned or permanently suspended — often without warning.

Agency clients get access to Readyt's Anti-Ban Premium account pool. These are the platform's highest-tier accounts: they carry the longest warmup history, the most karma accumulated across a wide range of subreddits, and the most established post and comment records. Operating through these accounts significantly reduces the risk of moderation action and keeps your campaign running without interruption. The Readyt team manages account rotation, warmup maintenance, and compliance with subreddit-specific rules so you never have to think about it.
