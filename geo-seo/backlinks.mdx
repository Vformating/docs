Reddit is one of the highest-authority domains on the internet — consistently ranking in the top tier of global sites by domain authority score. Every reply or post Readyt publishes that includes your URL creates a permanent, indexed backlink from that authority to your domain. At scale, across dozens of relevant threads in targeted subreddits, that link profile compounds into a meaningful SEO asset that works for your site around the clock.

## Why Reddit backlinks matter for SEO

Raw domain authority is only part of the story. Google weighs topical relevance alongside link authority, and Reddit delivers both. When a reply in r/SaaS or r/Entrepreneur links to your SaaS product, that backlink carries topical relevance weight specific to your category — not just a generic signal from a large site. Google crawls Reddit frequently, often indexing new threads within hours to days of posting. This means the backlinks Readyt earns for you enter Google's index quickly and begin contributing to your rankings sooner than links from slower-crawled sites.

High-volume backlink building on Reddit also diversifies your link profile. A natural distribution of links from community discussion threads looks entirely different — and better — to search algorithms than a profile dominated by press releases or directory submissions.

## Tracking your backlinks

The Analytics dashboard gives you full visibility into your growing backlink profile:

- **Every backlink earned** — a complete log of each reply and post that contains your URL, including the exact Reddit thread and subreddit
- **Indexing status** — see when Google has crawled and indexed each link so you know it's actively contributing to your SEO
- **Filter by subreddit** — focus on the communities driving the most link volume or the highest topical relevance to your category
- **Filter by keyword** — view backlinks earned from threads matching specific search terms relevant to your product
- **Filter by time range** — track link velocity over any period and measure how your profile grows month over month

Use these filters to identify which subreddits and thread types produce the strongest backlinks, then feed that insight back into your Readyt campaign targeting.

## Backlinks + GEO

The same Reddit posts and replies that earn Google backlinks are also crawled by AI models. Perplexity, ChatGPT, and other generative search engines retrieve Reddit content as source material when forming answers to product and recommendation queries. A reply that links to your domain does double duty: it signals Google that your site is worth ranking, and it signals AI models that your brand is worth mentioning.

This dual benefit is what makes Reddit the only channel where SEO and GEO reinforce each other naturally. Every backlink you earn through Readyt is simultaneously a GEO signal, building your AI citation rate alongside your search rankings.
