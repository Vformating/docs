When a potential buyer asks ChatGPT "what's the best tool for X?" and your brand name appears in the answer, that is an AI citation — and it functions as a word-of-mouth recommendation at AI scale. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the discipline of earning those mentions systematically. Readyt tracks your brand's citation rate across every major AI model, shows you exactly where those citations originate, and helps you build the Reddit presence that drives them.

## Brand visibility vs. source visibility

GEO captures two distinct signals, and both matter for your growth.

**Brand visibility** measures how often your brand name appears directly in an AI model's generated answer. This is the most impactful outcome — the model is actively recommending you by name, for example: "You should try YourBrand for this use case." Brand visibility translates directly to new top-of-funnel demand from buyers who never searched for you.

**Source visibility** measures how often your domain is listed as a cited source the AI used to construct its answer — a footnote, reference link, or attribution shown alongside the response. Source visibility signals that the AI model treats your content as authoritative and trustworthy, which reinforces brand visibility over time and reflects well on your SEO standing.

Optimizing for both is the goal: brand visibility drives immediate recommendations, while source visibility builds the underlying trust infrastructure that sustains them.

## How the tracking works

Readyt runs a continuous LLM evaluation pipeline on your behalf. The system sends probe queries — based on your product category, use case keywords, and target audience — across every supported AI model. It then parses each response and attributes every mention to your brand name or domain. You see the results in the GEO dashboard, organized by model, mention type, and trend over time. The pipeline refreshes every four hours, so you always have a current view of where you stand in AI-generated answers.

## How Reddit drives citations

AI models including Perplexity and ChatGPT crawl Reddit as a primary retrieval source when forming answers to product recommendation and comparison queries. Reddit's volume, recency, and community credibility make it one of the most-indexed sources for this category of question. When your brand appears consistently in relevant subreddit threads — in helpful, contextual replies rather than promotional noise — AI models recognize it as an answer worth surfacing.

Readyt's reply engine targets the exact threads where buyers ask for tool and product recommendations. Every approved reply in those threads is a signal that accumulates in your GEO profile. The more consistently you participate in relevant subreddits, the more frequently AI models encounter your brand when retrieving material to answer those same questions.
