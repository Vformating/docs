## Why Reddit compounds

Reddit is not a one-shot channel — it builds on itself. Your account history and karma accumulate over time, making each successive reply carry more weight within the communities you participate in. As your presence grows, subreddit members develop familiarity with your brand before they ever visit your site. That familiarity accelerates conversion when buyers do reach out.

The compounding effect extends beyond Reddit's own community. AI models — including ChatGPT and Perplexity — increasingly treat Reddit as a high-quality retrieval source when forming responses to product and tool recommendation queries. The longer your brand has been consistently present in relevant threads, the deeper and broader that signal becomes. A corpus of helpful, authentic replies in your target subreddits is an asset that grows in value every week it sits on Reddit's servers. The moat widens the earlier you start building it.

## Timeframes to expect

Expect to see qualified Reddit conversations start within your first week of activity — high-intent threads move fast, and Readyt surfaces them the moment they appear. Google typically indexes Reddit backlinks within days to a few weeks depending on crawl frequency for the subreddit. AI citations take the longest to build but deliver some of the highest-value outcomes: based on real customer results, brands that maintain consistent Reddit activity begin appearing in ChatGPT and Perplexity responses within six to eight weeks.
