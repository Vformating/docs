## Get started in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [readyt.ai](https://readyt.ai/pricing) and choose a plan. No setup call required — Readyt starts tracking within 5 minutes of connecting your brand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your brand">
    Enter your product URL, add competitor names, and set your target keywords. Readyt immediately begins scanning Reddit threads for matching intent signals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and approve replies">
    Readyt surfaces high-intent threads and drafts replies in your voice. Spend 15 minutes a day reviewing — approve in one click or edit before posting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track your results">
    Monitor Reddit leads, Google backlinks, and AI citations from the Analytics dashboard. Every result is traced back to the exact thread that earned it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
