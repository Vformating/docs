FAQs

Things people ask before they sign up.

How do I get started with Readyt?

Sign up, connect your brand and competitors, and Readyt starts tracking AI citations and Reddit threads within 5 minutes. No setup call needed.

Can I segment visibility by model, region, or audience?

Yes. Filter by AI model (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Grok), geographic region, or audience segment. Custom segments available on Pro.

How often is the data refreshed?

AI citations refresh every 4 hours. Reddit data is real-time via websockets. Sentiment recomputed daily.

What are brand visibility vs. source visibility metrics?

Brand visibility = how often your brand is mentioned in AI responses. Source visibility = how often your domain/content is cited as a source by AI. Both matter, both tracked.

How do source citations work behind the scenes?

Citations are extracted from AI model responses via our LLM evaluation pipeline. We run probe queries across every model, parse responses, and attribute each mention back to your brand or domain.

How does the MCP integration work with Claude and Cursor?

Install the Readyt MCP server in Claude or Cursor. Then query your brand visibility, citations, and competitors directly from your AI tool. Full docs at readyt.ai/mcp.
