Reddit's community culture is unforgiving toward promotional content — a post that reads like an ad gets downvoted, reported, or removed within minutes. Readyt's Post Generator solves this by writing posts the way a genuine community member would: opinionated, helpful, and free of marketing language. It handles subreddit selection, timing, and formatting automatically, so your brand earns organic reach and Reddit SEO credit without your team having to learn the culture of every subreddit you target.

## Writing native posts

Readyt generates posts using your brand voice configuration combined with a deep understanding of how each subreddit communicates. It doesn't just avoid buzzwords — it actively mirrors the vocabulary, format, and community norms of the specific subreddit it's writing for.

A post targeted at r/SaaS reads differently from one aimed at r/startups or r/devops. Readyt accounts for those differences automatically: the length, the use of headings or bullet points, whether personal anecdotes land better than structured comparisons, and what the subreddit's moderators flag as spam. The result is a post that contributes to the community rather than interrupting it.

You configure your brand voice once in **Settings → Brand Voice**, covering your tone adjectives, topics you can speak credibly about, products or features you want to highlight, and any language you want the generator to avoid. Every post Readyt writes draws from that configuration, so output stays consistent across subreddits and team members.

## Subreddit selection

Readyt selects target subreddits by cross-referencing your ICP definition, your keyword list, and an analysis of where your competitors are active and gaining traction. It surfaces subreddits where your ideal customers are already having the conversations you want to join.

You can run up to 12 subreddits simultaneously in your posting queue. For each one, Readyt tracks post performance, moderator sensitivity, and community engagement patterns — adjusting future post frequency and format if a subreddit starts showing signs of fatigue.

To manage your subreddit list, go to **Post Generator → Subreddits**. From there you can:

- **Add a subreddit** manually by name if you want to target somewhere Readyt hasn't suggested
- **Remove a subreddit** to pause posting there without losing its performance history
- **Override a suggestion** if Readyt's recommendation doesn't fit your current campaign focus

Subreddit suggestions refresh weekly based on updated keyword trends and competitor activity, so your targeting evolves as the market does.

## Peak-hour scheduling

Posting at the wrong time on Reddit is nearly as costly as posting the wrong content — a post that lands when the subreddit is quiet gets buried before it earns any momentum. Readyt analyzes the historical activity patterns of each subreddit in your queue and schedules posts for the windows when your target audience is most likely to be online and engaged.

Scheduling runs automatically. When you approve a post (or if auto-post is enabled), Readyt queues it for the next optimal posting window rather than publishing immediately. You can see the scheduled time in your post queue and adjust it manually if you have a time-sensitive reason to post earlier or later.

Karma compounds over time: consistently well-timed posts from a healthy account build upvote momentum, which improves how Reddit's algorithm treats future posts from the same account. This is one of the core reasons account warming and post scheduling work together — both are investments in long-term organic reach.

## SEO and GEO optimization

Every Reddit post you publish through Readyt is a permanent, indexable page on one of the internet's highest-authority domains. Reddit backlinks are followed by Google and carry genuine ranking weight, particularly for informational and comparison queries in B2B software categories. Over time, a consistent posting strategy builds a library of backlinks that strengthens your domain's authority in organic search.

Beyond traditional SEO, Readyt optimizes posts for **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** — the emerging practice of ensuring your content is crawled and cited by AI models like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini. These models index Reddit heavily when constructing answers to product and category questions. A well-written Reddit post from a credible account can surface in AI-generated responses months after it was published, driving brand and source visibility you can track directly in Readyt's Analytics dashboard.

Readyt's Post Generator structures posts to maximize both forms of visibility: clean formatting for crawler readability, substantive content that AI models are likely to cite, and natural keyword density that aligns with how your buyers search.
