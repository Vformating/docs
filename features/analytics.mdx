Readyt's Analytics dashboard gives you a complete picture of what your Reddit activity is actually producing — not just engagement vanity metrics, but real attribution: which threads drove leads, which posts earned backlinks, and which AI model responses are citing your brand or domain. Every data point connects back to a specific Reddit thread, so you can see exactly which content is working and double down on what's moving the needle.

## Brand visibility vs. source visibility

These two metrics are distinct and tracked separately in your dashboard.

**Brand visibility** measures how often your brand name appears in responses generated by AI models — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, and Grok. When a user asks one of these models a product or category question and the model mentions your brand by name in its answer, that's a brand visibility event. It reflects how embedded your brand has become in the AI models' understanding of your category.

**Source visibility** measures how often your domain or specific content (Reddit posts, your website, linked resources) is cited as a source by AI models when they construct answers. A source citation means the model didn't just mention your brand — it pointed to your content as evidence. Source visibility is a stronger signal of authoritative positioning and is particularly valuable for SEO-adjacent brand building.

Both metrics compound over time. A single well-performing Reddit post can generate brand and source visibility events for months after it was published, as AI models continuously re-crawl and re-evaluate their training and retrieval sources.

## Filtering and segmentation

Your Analytics dashboard lets you slice visibility and performance data along three primary dimensions:

**AI model** — filter results to a specific model (e.g., show only Perplexity citations, or compare ChatGPT vs. Claude brand mention rates side by side). Use this to identify which models your buyers are most likely using and where you need to improve your presence.

**Geographic region** — filter by country or region to understand where your Reddit content is driving AI visibility. If you're expanding into a new market, regional filtering tells you whether your content is gaining traction there before you commit more budget.

**Audience segment** — filter by the audience segments you've defined in your ICP configuration. See whether your brand visibility is growing with founder-led companies, enterprise buyers, or a specific vertical.

Custom audience segments are available on the **Pro plan**. Starter and Growth plans include Readyt's default segments based on subreddit category and intent score bands.

## Data refresh rates

Different data types in your dashboard update on different schedules based on how they're sourced:

| Data type            | Refresh rate                        |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| AI citations         | Every 4 hours                       |
| Reddit thread data   | Real-time via websockets            |
| Sentiment analysis   | Recomputed daily                    |

Reddit thread data — new replies, upvotes, comment counts — streams into your dashboard in real time via a persistent websocket connection. AI citation data is collected by querying each supported model and attributing mentions and source citations to your brand or domain, so it refreshes on a 4-hour cycle. Sentiment analysis is a more compute-intensive process, so it updates once per day, typically in the early morning UTC window.

<Info>
  Citation data is collected automatically across every supported AI model — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, and Grok — and mentions and source citations are attributed to your brand or domain. This process runs continuously in the background; no configuration is required on your end.
</Info>

## Thread-level attribution

Every lead, backlink, and AI citation in your dashboard is traceable to a specific Reddit thread. Click any data point in the Analytics view and Readyt shows you the exact thread that generated it — the subreddit, the original post, the reply Readyt posted, and the downstream events that followed.

This level of attribution lets you answer questions that most marketing teams can't: which reply drove that inbound lead? Which post is being cited by Perplexity when someone asks about your category? Which subreddit is producing the most SEO-value backlinks this month?

Use thread-level attribution to audit your content strategy and feed insights back into your Post Generator and Reply Engine configuration. If a particular thread format or subreddit is consistently outperforming others, you can prioritize it directly in your campaign settings.

## Sentiment analysis

Available on **Growth and above** plans, sentiment analysis tells you whether the Reddit conversations mentioning your brand are positive, neutral, or negative — and how that sentiment is trending over time.

Readyt evaluates sentiment at the thread level, not just the keyword level. A thread that mentions your brand in a comparison post might be neutral overall but contain highly positive comments from existing users — sentiment analysis surfaces both the top-line trend and the nuance underneath it.

Use sentiment data to:

- **Catch negative threads early** before they gain upvote momentum and become reputation risks
- **Identify advocates** — users who are consistently positive about your brand and might be worth engaging directly
- **Measure campaign impact** — track whether a new reply campaign is shifting sentiment in a target subreddit over a 30-day window

Sentiment scores update daily and are visible both in the main dashboard summary and at the individual thread level in the attribution view.
