This quickstart walks you through everything you need to go from a blank account to approving your first AI-drafted Reddit reply. No setup call required, no Reddit expertise needed — by the end of this guide, Readyt will be actively monitoring threads where your buyers are talking right now, and you'll have posted your first reply from a warmed account.

<Tip>
  Readyt starts surfacing threads within 5 minutes. Most users approve their first reply in under 15 minutes.
</Tip>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to [readyt.ai/pricing](https://readyt.ai/pricing) and choose the plan that fits your team size and reply volume. Starter gives you 1 seat and 150 replies per month. Growth adds a second seat and 300 replies. Pro unlocks 3 seats and 1,000 replies per month. If you're an agency managing multiple brands, select Agency to talk to the team about a custom plan.

    After picking a plan, create your account with your work email. You'll land directly in the Readyt dashboard — no onboarding call, no form to fill out before you can explore.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your brand">
    Enter your product's URL in the Brand Profile section. Readyt uses this URL to track backlinks it generates on your behalf and to anchor its understanding of what your product does.

    Next, write a short brand description — two or three sentences about what your product does, who it's for, and what makes it different. This description shapes how the AI Reply Engine matches your tone and positions your product in replies.

    Finally, add three to five competitor names or URLs. Readyt immediately begins monitoring threads where those competitors are mentioned, which are often the highest-intent opportunities for a well-placed, helpful reply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add keywords">
    Add five to ten intent keywords — the phrases your ideal buyers type into Reddit's search bar when they're actively looking for a solution. Think in terms of buying-stage language rather than feature language.

    Strong examples include:
    - `"best [your category] tool for startups"`
    - `"looking for a [solution type] that does X"`
    - `"alternatives to [competitor name]"`
    - `"[competitor name] too expensive"`
    - `"[your category] recommendations"`

    Avoid generic single-word keywords. The more specific the phrase, the more precisely Readyt can match threads where a real purchase decision is underway. You can always add or remove keywords later as you learn which ones surface the best opportunities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your first lead opportunities">
    Within five minutes of saving your brand profile and keywords, Readyt populates your Opportunities feed with matching Reddit threads. Each thread displays an intent score from 1 to 100.

    A high intent score means Readyt's LLM pipeline detected clear buyer signals in the post — the person is actively evaluating solutions, comparing tools, or asking for recommendations. Threads scoring above 70 are typically the highest-priority replies to write. Start there.

    Click any thread to read the original post, see which keyword or competitor trigger surfaced it, and preview the AI-drafted reply Readyt has already prepared for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve your first reply">
    Open a high-intent thread and read Readyt's draft reply. The AI has already matched the tone of the subreddit and positioned your product in a way that fits naturally into the conversation.

    Choose a reply tone to adjust the voice:
    - **Helpful** — leads with value, mentions your product as one option among useful context
    - **Direct** — confident recommendation, more product-forward
    - **Story** — first-person framing, describes a relatable scenario before introducing your product

    Once you're satisfied with the reply, click **Approve**. Readyt posts it immediately from one of your warmed Reddit accounts — accounts that already have karma history and established activity patterns so the reply lands credibly. You'll see the live Reddit link appear in your dashboard within seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>
